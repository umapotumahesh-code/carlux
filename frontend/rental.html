Frequently Asked Questions

What documents do I need for car rental pickup?

You need to bring the following documents during pickup:

  • Valid Driving License - Original or DigiLocker (minimum 1 year old)
  • ID Proof - Aadhaar, PAN Card, or Passport (same as booking)
  • Credit/Debit Card - For security deposit authorization
  • Booking Confirmation - Screenshot or printout of booking details

Note: Photocopies or A4 printouts of licenses are not accepted.

How much is the security deposit and when will it be refunded?

Security deposit details:

  • Amount: ₹2,000 - ₹10,000 (varies by car model)
  • Method: Pre-authorization on credit/debit card (no actual charge)
  • Refund Timeline: 7-10 working days after safe vehicle return
  • Deductions: Only for damages, traffic fines, or fuel shortfall

The deposit is fully refundable if the car is returned in the same condition.

What happens if I return the car with less fuel?

Fuel policy details:

  • Requirement: Return car with same fuel level as received
  • Shortfall Charge: ₹500 refueling service fee + actual fuel cost
  • Measurement: Based on fuel gauge reading at pickup/return
  • Recommendation: Take photos of fuel gauge during pickup
Can I extend my rental period or return early?

Rental period modifications:

  • Extension: Contact support at least 2 hours before original return time
  • Extension Charges: Hourly rates apply (₹200-500/hour based on car)
  • Early Return: No refund for unused time, but no penalty charges
  • Late Return: ₹200/hour penalty after 30-minute grace period
Can I travel to other states or specific restricted areas?

Travel restrictions and permissions:

  • Interstate Travel: Allowed with prior approval (contact support)
  • Restricted Areas: Leh/Ladakh, Kaza/Nako, Spiti Valley are strictly prohibited
  • Border Areas: Some border regions require special permits
  • Violation Penalty: Full rental amount + additional charges

Always inform us about your travel plans during booking or pickup.

What should I do if the car breaks down or has an accident?

Emergency procedures:

  • Breakdown: Call our 24/7 helpline immediately: 1800-123-7368
  • Accident: Ensure safety first, then call police + our support
  • Roadside Assistance: Free towing and emergency repairs provided
  • Replacement Car: Available if repair time exceeds 4 hours
  • Documentation: Take photos and file police report for insurance
What activities are prohibited in the rental car?

Strictly prohibited activities:

  • Smoking: Absolutely no smoking inside the vehicle
  • Alcohol: No consumption of alcohol inside the car
  • Commercial Use: No taxi, delivery, or commercial services
  • Racing/Stunts: No racing, rallying, or dangerous driving
  • Overloading: Respect seating and weight limits
  • Pets: Prior approval required for pet travel

Violation may result in booking termination and penalty charges.

What payment methods are accepted and when is payment due?

Payment information:

  • Booking Payment: Online via credit/debit card, UPI, or net banking
  • Security Deposit: Credit/debit card pre-authorization only
  • Additional Charges: Paid during return (cash or card)
  • Refunds: Processed to original payment method
  • International Cards: Accepted for NRI customers

Still have questions?
