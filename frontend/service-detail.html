Frequently Asked Questions

We offer comprehensive car services including regular maintenance (oil changes, filter replacements), brake services, engine diagnostics and repair, AC service, battery replacement, tire services, electrical repairs, and complete car wash packages. Our certified technicians use genuine parts and modern equipment for all services.

You can book appointments through our online booking system, phone call, or by visiting our service center. We're open Monday to Saturday from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM, and Sunday from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM. For urgent services, we also offer 24/7 emergency roadside assistance.

Our service charges vary based on the type of service and car model. Basic services start from ₹500, major services from ₹2,000, and specialized repairs are quoted after diagnosis. We accept cash, all major credit/debit cards, UPI, and digital wallets. Free estimates are provided before any major work.

Yes, we provide warranty on all our services and parts. Service warranty ranges from 1-6 months depending on the type of work. Genuine parts come with manufacturer warranty (6-12 months), while aftermarket parts have 3-6 months warranty. All warranty terms are clearly mentioned in your service receipt.

Yes, we offer convenient doorstep services for basic maintenance like oil changes, battery replacement, and minor repairs. For major services, we provide free pickup and drop facility within 10km radius. Doorstep services are available on weekdays with advance booking, and pickup/drop is available all days.

We use only genuine OEM parts for newer vehicles under warranty and high-quality aftermarket parts for older vehicles. All parts are sourced from authorized dealers and reputable manufacturers. We also offer customers the choice between genuine and aftermarket parts with transparent pricing for both options.

Yes, we provide 24/7 emergency breakdown services including jump-start, flat tire change, emergency fuel delivery, lockout assistance, and towing services. Our emergency response team typically reaches within 30-45 minutes in the city. Emergency service charges apply based on time and location.

Yes, we are authorized service partners with major insurance companies. We handle the entire insurance claim process including damage assessment, estimate preparation, claim filing, and direct billing with insurance companies. Our team ensures hassle-free claim settlement and quality repairs.
